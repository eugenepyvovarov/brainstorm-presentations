SlopCodeBench-style challenges
Requirements arrive over checkpoints
Same codebase must evolve issue-by-issue
Not the whole problem dumped up front
Strict pass is the bar
New work green + old regressions still green
Early defects poison later checkpoints
Held-out black-box tests, not model-as-judge
Frontier scores still low
Published bench unsaturated (~teens %)
Even latest models fail full easy tracks
Most wins are early checkpoints only
More spend buys correctness — not enough of it
Deterministic “slop” signals
Size, complexity, duplication, deps — computed not judged
Complexity & verbosity climb over checkpoints
Useful directionally; not a full maintainability oracle
What a good oracle looks like
Multi-checkpoint strict pass ≈ real factory work
Better than one-shot SWE-bench alone
Better than another model rating “clean”
Amplify with transfer
Strong model builds early checkpoints
Weaker model must implement the next one
If the small model can extend it, design stayed changeable
When lights-off might be defensible
High scores on held-out long-horizon benches
Having that signal matters more than guessing the date
Until then: keep human steering
