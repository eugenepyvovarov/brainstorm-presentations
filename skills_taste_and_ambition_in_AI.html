Reply to “AI equalized everyone” Homogenization is real; conclusion is wrong
2. Where AI actually is Superhuman in spots; humans still steer
2. Where AI actually is Superhuman in spots; humans still steer

SI would beat humans across domains + strategic agency. Today: superhuman code/recall; subhuman autonomy, physical world, research, taste. We still direct the tool.

Superhuman: code + recall Not full superintelligence yet
Still weak: long-horizon autonomy Physical world, research, taste
Humans still hold the wheel Attention and judgment throttle everything
X bias: oversamples vibe apps Undersamples deep tech / enterprise
3. Why skill still wins Floor rose; ceiling and taste remain
3. Why skill still wins Floor rose; ceiling and taste remain

Coder grief is fair; “skills are dead” isn’t. AI amplifies you. If quality still varies, skill differentiates. Floor rose more than ceiling — gradient compressed, not gone. Photo/digital camera: everyone shoots; few make Nat Geo.

AI amplifies skill, doesn’t erase it Quality gradient still exists
Floor up a lot, ceiling less Differentiation compressed, not deleted
Apps-in-hours is half true Scaffold ≠ reliability / compliance / support
Photo analogy: tools equalize capture Not taste, judgment, or timing
4. What’s commoditized Generation layer dies; moats live
4. What’s commoditized Generation layer dies; moats live

Generation layer → margins toward zero. Moats that survive: distribution, brand, trust, networks, data, switching costs, regulation, capital, embeddedness. Copy code ≠ copy users. Speed-to-trend is temporary. Labs fight commoditization too (model layer).

Commoditized: generation / code scaffolds Anyone can clone a surface app
Survives: distribution, brand, trust, data Networks, switching costs, regulation…
2-hour app often worth ~$0 Slop burns attention and distribution
Labs aren’t sole winners They compete on the model layer too
5. What to do instead Complexity × excellence × ambition
5. What to do instead Complexity × excellence × ambition

Real gold rush = wield enormous complexity × excellence. Year on one insane bet with full AI leverage — not another clone app. Heuristic: if it doesn’t sound a bit crazy and scare you, aim higher.

Opportunity: complexity × excellence Not faster weather apps
Commit a year + max AI leverage On something the world needs
Think bigger than “an app” Poverty, Earth twin, generative engineering…
Heuristic: must scare you a bit Safe ideas commoditize overnight
Gone building Back when there’s something to show
